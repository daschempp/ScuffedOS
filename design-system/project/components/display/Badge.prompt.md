Small pill for status, categories, and counts.

```jsx
<Badge color="green" dot>On track</Badge>
<Badge color="clay">Over budget</Badge>
<Badge color="sky" icon={<i data-lucide="mic" />}>Voice note</Badge>
```

Colors: `neutral`, `green`, `clay`, `honey`, `sky`, `plum`, `solid`. `dot` adds a status dot; `icon` adds a leading glyph.
